What training covers

The same skills, taught at the right level.

A first-grader and an eighth-grader are working on the same game. The difference is pace, complexity, and how much we ask them to figure out on their own.

  • Ball handling
  • Footwork
  • Finishing
  • Shooting
  • Decision-making
  • Basketball IQ
  • Game-transfer skills
  • Confidence

Training is available for players approximately age 5 and up, depending on the session. Groups are kept small so coaches can correct, teach and actually watch each player work.

Weekly skills training

Year-round skill development.

Triumph coaches run skills training throughout the year. Sessions are built around fundamentals and game-transfer work, and the group a player joins depends on age, experience and what they're working toward.

Coach Oli currently leads many weekly sessions. Coach Marlowe's availability and training model may expand seasonally.

Sunday development training

Three sessions. Three levels.

Sunday is Triumph's development block. Players are grouped by readiness rather than only by age, so nobody is stuck in a session that's too easy or too far ahead.

About Early Foundations

The 11:00 session is for very new players, on lower rims. The emphasis is fun, comfort with the ball, movement and early exposure to the game. Foundational habits get built quietly underneath — we're not drilling technical footwork with five-year-olds.

Help young players fall in love with playing.

Not sure which session?

Send us your player's age and experience and we'll tell you which group fits. Older beginners in particular usually shouldn't be grouped with much younger new players — the 1:00 session exists partly for them.
